Hill country drives that wash out after a hard rain

Guernsey County's terrain rolls and climbs, and drives on hills take the worst of every rainstorm. Water cuts ruts down the slope and carries gravel into the ditch. We regrade and crown drives, add cross-culverts where the water crosses, and build a base that sheds runoff instead of carving channels. On steeper grades we add drainage tile and check dams.

Pads and drainage for outbuildings, shops and acreage

Out toward Byesville and Lore City, much of the property is family acreage where people add a shop or pole barn beside the house. Clay and shale don't give you a flat pad to start with, so we cut and fill, compact a solid base and grade for positive drainage before anything goes up. A pad that holds water isn't level for long — we plan the water path first.

Old farm lanes, culverts and tile that need reopening

Working farms around Cambridge have lanes put in decades ago — some still functional, some caved-in at the culvert or silted up at the tile. We locate the old tile lines and culverts, clean or replace them, and regrade the lane so it drains again.

Excavation questions Cambridge landowners ask us

My driveway washes out every spring on the hill — can you fix it permanently?

The fix isn't more gravel on top; it's reworking the base with a crown and cross-culverts where the water sheets across. On steeper grades we add drainage tile and check dams so water never gets the speed to carve ruts.

How does all the clay and shale under our ground affect building a shop pad?

Clay holds water and shale makes a firm base, so the key is grading the pad so water runs off and the base stays dry. We cut into the slope, compact in lifts and grade for positive drainage. A pad on clay and shale works fine once the water's planned for.

We have an old farm lane with caved-in culverts — can you bring it back?

That's a common job in Guernsey County. We locate the old culverts and tile lines, dig them out, reset or replace the pipe, and regrade the lane with proper crown and drainage. Most old lanes can be reopened in a day or two.

Can you clear and grade a building site on acreage outside Byesville?

Yes. We clear the site, cut a level pad, and put in the drainage a hillside or field-edge build needs. We work acreage throughout Guernsey County and plan the access and water flow before we ever start cutting.
