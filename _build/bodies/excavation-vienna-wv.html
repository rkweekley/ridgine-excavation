Tight lots and suburban ground

Vienna is a mix — established neighborhoods with mature trees and fenced yards next to newer builds, with alley access on the older blocks and side yards too tight for equipment. The ground tends toward clay that holds water, and on the lower end of town toward the Ohio the water table sits high enough to matter when you're cutting a foundation.

For in-town jobs we bring machines sized to the access — a compact excavator that fits through a gate or down an alley. We plan the in-and-out route before we start so curbs, fences, and the neighbor's yard stay the way we found them.

Drainage, driveways, and the acreage beyond town

Plenty of Vienna yards have a drainage problem the owner has lived with — a low spot that holds water after every rain, a downspout dumping next to the foundation, a side yard that turns into a creek when it storms. Grading is usually the fix: reshape the ground so water runs where it should, adding a French drain or swale if the lot calls for it.

Out past the city limits toward Williamstown and Mineral Wells, the lots open into acreage — clearing wooded parcels, cutting driveways into raw ground, and prepping building sites. We do the full sequence from clearing and rough grade through final pad.

Questions we hear around Vienna

Can you get equipment into a backyard with only alley access?

Almost always. Older Vienna blocks have alleyways, and we run compact machines that fit through gates and narrow passes. We'll assess access during the estimate so there are no surprises.

My yard floods every time it rains — can grading actually fix that?

In most cases, yes. We regrade the low points so water runs toward the street or a drainage path. If the lot is flat with nowhere for water to go, we add a French drain or a dry swale.

What about the high water table near the river bottoms?

On the lower side of Vienna the water table sits close to the surface. We check it before we cut, and if it's a problem we raise the pad or add drainage.

Do you cross the river and work in Ohio too?

Yes. We work both sides of the Ohio — Parkersburg, Vienna, and Williamstown on the West Virginia side, and Belpre, Marietta, and Lowell on the Ohio side.
