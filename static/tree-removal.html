Removing Trees With the Ground in Mind

Cutting a tree down is only half the job. The stump and the root ball underneath are what keep you from mowing, building, or planting where the tree used to be. We take trees out with the roots, grind the stumps down below grade, and leave the spot ready for whatever comes next — grass, a driveway, a new structure, or just clean open ground.

It matters most on the wooded ridge lots common around the Mid-Ohio Valley, where a line of trees and roots often stands between you and usable acreage. Clearing the stumps, not just the trunks, is what turns that ground into something you can actually work.

Hazard Trees and Storm-Damaged Timber

A dead or leaning tree next to a house, barn, or power line is a job you don't want to put off. We take them down methodically, piece by piece when they're close to a structure, so nothing comes down where it shouldn't. Because we run 24/7 emergency response, storm-toppled trees and urgent hazards can be handled quickly instead of left to wait.

Common Tree Removal Questions

Do you remove the stump too, or just the tree?

We handle both. We can take the tree and grind the stump below grade in the same visit, so you're left with flat, mowable ground instead of a trip hazard.

Can you take down a tree leaning toward my house?

Yes. Trees close to structures come down in controlled pieces, so branches and sections are lowered rather than felled. It takes a little longer, but it protects your home and anything around the tree.

What happens to the brush and debris?

It leaves with us. We clean up the brush, chip what can be chipped, and haul away the debris so you aren't left with a pile to burn or dispose of yourself.

Do you clear fence lines?

Yes. Overgrown fence lines are one of our most common cleanup jobs — we clear the trees and brush along the property line so your fence can be reached, repaired, or rebuilt.
